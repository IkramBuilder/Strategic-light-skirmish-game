How to play

Goal: destroy every enemy structure.

Economy: workers mine blue crystal and return it to your HQ. Units stand on glowing relays to capture them; each relay adds energy, which advanced units, upgrades and your commander power need.

Counters: Troopers and Ravagers shred light units. Lancers and Spitters punish armored. Walkers and Behemoths crush structures and clumps.

Air: Gunships and Gargoyles fly over everything. Only units that hit air (Troopers, Spitters, flyers, heroes, casters, turrets and the Skyguard / Sporeback anti-air) can shoot them.

Support: Medics heal, Scouts capture relays twice as fast, Casters spend mana on a spell button, and one Hero per side boosts nearby allies.

Map events: meteor showers (leave gold), supply drops (first unit to reach claims +150 crystal), an Ancient Colossus boss (+500 crystal when slain) and crystal blooms at the centre when home fields run low.

LAN: press LAN multiplayer, enter your name, then Host or Join. Direct mode works with only a browser; Server mode uses server.js on a PC.

Modes: 1v1 on standard maps, or 3v3 (you plus AI allies P3 and P5) and 6-player Free-for-All on large maps with an 80 supply cap per player. Each player has a colour light under their units.

New races: Templars drop units by pod at a rally near their buildings. Warband packs of 5+ enrage. Legion warriors may reassemble once. Cult sacrifices units for a Fiend.

Aether Covenant: every unit and building has a blue shield that regenerates after 6 seconds out of combat. Structures (except HQ and Obelisks) must be inside an Obelisk power field. Units warp in at the Obelisk nearest your rally point.

Maps & expansions: every map has extra crystal fields. Build a new HQ next to them so workers mine nearby. Gold crystal gives 8 per trip.

Graphics: Low for older phones, Medium for most, High adds smoother models, extra parts and effects.

Sound: tap ♪ to mute. Volume and smooth mode are in the pause menu.

Fast thumb controls: hold on the map then drag to box-select; hold and release to grab all army around your finger. Buttons 1-3 are control groups: hold to save, tap to select, double-tap to jump the camera. The right-side buttons select all HQs, Barracks, Factories, Airfields or Academies at once (double-tap to jump).

Touch: tap a unit to select, double-tap to grab all of that type, tap ground to attack-move. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Use Box to drag-select, Move to retreat without fighting.

Mouse: left-drag box select, right-click to command, A then click to attack-move, S to stop, Q for your power, arrow keys to pan, wheel to zoom, Esc to cancel.

LAN multiplayer

Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi. Direct pairs two devices with a code or QR. Server needs one PC running node server.js; everyone opens the address it prints.
